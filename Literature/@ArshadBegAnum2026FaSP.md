Background: Artificial Intelligence (AI), with its range of applications and ease of use, is transforming medicine. AI usage is increasing exponentially in a resource-limited country like Pakistan; however, application in medical education is evolving and needs to be understood. Little work has been done on perceptions among faculty and students to understand their understanding and readiness to use AI, while keeping in mind the ethical issues it may raise. Objective: To explore perceptions of faculty and students regarding integration of AI into undergraduate medical education, potential challenges and ethical considerations in adoption of AI, at a public sector university in Pakistan. Methods: Exploratory descriptive qualitative study at Dow University of Health Sciences, Karachi, from October 2025 to March 2026, including faculty in-depth interviews (IDI) and focus group discussions (FGD) with MBBS students till data saturation was achieved.
